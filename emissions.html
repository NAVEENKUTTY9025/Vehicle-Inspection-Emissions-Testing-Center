What We Test

Our state-of-the-art equipment and certified technicians ensure your vehicle meets all emissions standards quickly and accurately.

Emissions Test Types

Different vehicles require different testing methods. We handle them all.

OBD-II Testing

For 1996 and newer vehicles. We connect to your vehicle's onboard computer to read emissions-related diagnostic codes and readiness monitors.

Tailpipe Testing

For older model years. We measure actual exhaust gas concentrations using calibrated analyzers to determine compliance.

Diesel Opacity Testing

For diesel vehicles. We measure exhaust smoke opacity using state-approved meters to ensure your diesel meets regulations.

Possible Exemptions

Some vehicles may be exempt from emissions testing. Check if yours qualifies.

Newer Vehicles

Vehicles within their first 2–3 model years may be exempt depending on state regulations.

Electric Vehicles

Fully electric vehicles (EVs) do not produce tailpipe emissions and are typically exempt.

Classic / Antique

Vehicles 25+ years old with historic registration often qualify for emissions exemptions.
