General Inspection & Visit Questions

Walk-ins are always welcome! Over 90% of our customers pull directly into our drive-thru lanes without an appointment and are finished in under 15 minutes. However, online booking is available if you prefer a guaranteed timeslot for commercial fleets or specialty vehicles.
Please bring your current vehicle registration document (or state DMV renewal notification), proof of active auto insurance, and a valid photo ID. If you need an out-of-state VIN verification, also bring the vehicle title.
Standard safety inspections take approximately 10–15 minutes. Computerized emissions tests take 8–10 minutes. If performing both together, total drive-thru time is typically around 15–18 minutes.
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), mobile NFC payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay), and cash. Corporate accounts with monthly consolidated billing are available for fleet customers.

Vehicle Safety Inspection Standards

Our state-licensed technicians inspect brake pads & hydraulic lines, tire tread depth (must exceed 2/32"), headlight aim and signal bulbs, steering linkage, ball joints, shock absorbers, windshield condition, wiper operation, horn, seatbelts, and chassis integrity.
A chip or small crack outside the primary driver wiper sweep area is generally permissible. However, cracks directly obstructing the driver's forward field of view or cracks radiating across the glass surface fail state highway safety guidelines.
You will receive a certified rejection receipt detailing the exact mechanical fault. You have 30 days to repair the item at any repair shop of your choice and return to Apex Inspect for a free re-inspection of the failed item.

Emissions & Clean Air Standards

No. Under EPA and state emissions statutes, an illuminated Check Engine (MIL) light is an automatic test failure. The underlying fault code must be diagnosed and repaired, and the internal vehicle drive cycle monitors reset to "Ready" status prior to re-testing.
When a car battery is disconnected or dead, the vehicle's computer erases its internal emissions readiness monitors. To restore readiness, drive your car for 50–100 miles over 2–3 days in both city and highway conditions before returning for your free retest.
Our emissions analyzers are directly integrated with the State Department of Motor Vehicles computer system. Passing certificates are uploaded electronically within 60 seconds of completion, allowing you to renew your registration online immediately.

VIN Verification & Out-of-State Titling

A certified VIN verification is required whenever you bring an out-of-state vehicle into the state for initial registration, transfer an out-of-state title, register an imported vehicle, or register a custom-built kit car.
The inspector verifies the dashboard windshield VIN plate, the federal safety certification label on the driver's door jamb, and secondary frame/engine stampings to ensure all identification numbers correspond exactly with your title documents.
