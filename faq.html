General Questions

Safety Inspections

Our safety inspection checks brakes, lights, tires, steering, suspension, windshield, wipers, horn, mirrors, seat belts, and other state-mandated safety equipment. A full checklist is available at any of our stations.
You'll receive a detailed report of the issues found. You can then make repairs at a shop of your choice and return for a free retest within 30 days.
Requirements vary by state and vehicle type. Most personal vehicles require an annual or biennial inspection. Check your state DMV website or contact us for specifics.

Emissions Testing

Testing frequency varies by state and vehicle age. Most vehicles require testing every 1–2 years. Newer vehicles may be exempt for the first 2–3 model years.
Exemptions vary by model year and vehicle type. Generally, newer vehicles (within 2–3 years), fully electric vehicles, and some classic/antique vehicles may be exempt. Check our emissions page or contact us.
OBD-II testing (for 1996 and newer vehicles) connects to your vehicle's onboard computer to read diagnostic codes. Tailpipe testing (for older vehicles) measures actual exhaust gas concentrations. We handle both.
Yes — we electronically report all emissions test results directly to the DMV. You'll receive a printed certificate for your records, but you don't need to file anything yourself.

VIN & Documentation

VIN verification confirms that your vehicle's identification number matches its paperwork. It's required for out-of-state registrations, title transfers, and certain DMV transactions.
Bring your vehicle title (or applicable DMV forms), current registration if available, valid photo ID, and the vehicle itself. For out-of-state vehicles, bring any import or customs documentation.
Most VIN verifications take 10–15 minutes. We provide same-day paperwork for most vehicles, and can often submit results electronically to the DMV.
