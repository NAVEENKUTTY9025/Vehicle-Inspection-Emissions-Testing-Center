Inspection Scope

What We Inspect On Every Vehicle

Our state-licensed inspectors follow a strict 12-point standardized checklist to guarantee roadworthiness and passenger safety.

Braking System

Pads, rotors, hydraulic lines, emergency brake hold.

Emissions & OBD-II

Readiness monitors, catalytic converter, gas cap seal.

Tires & Tread

Minimum 2/32" depth, sidewall integrity, pressure.

Lighting & Signals

High/low beams, reverse lamps, hazards & turn signals.

Steering & Alignment

Tie rods, ball joints, power steering fluid & free play.

Glass & Windshield

Cracks outside driver sweep, wiper blades, washers.

Seatbelts & Airbags

Latching mechanism, retraction, SRS warning status.

Suspension System

Shock absorbers, struts, coil springs & bushings.

Fuel System Integrity

Fuel lines, evaporative canister, tank security.

Horn & Sound Device

Audible at 200 feet, steering wheel contact pad.

Body & Chassis Integrity

Structural rust, door latches, hood release catch.

VIN & State Records

Matching serial numbers, registration authenticity.

Help & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding state inspection mandates, emissions standards, and what you need to bring.

No appointment is required! Over 90% of our customers use our fast-track drive-thru lanes. Typical turnaround time is under 15 minutes from drive-in to departure. If you prefer a guaranteed slot for heavy-duty commercial fleets or imported vehicles, you may schedule online.
Please bring your current vehicle registration card (or renewal notice from the DMV), proof of valid automotive insurance, and your driver's license. If you are doing a VIN verification for an out-of-state vehicle, also bring the original vehicle title.
You will receive an itemized diagnostic failure printout explaining exactly what failed. Apex Inspect provides a 100% Free Retest within 30 days once you perform the necessary repairs.
The moment our certified technician finishes your test, our encrypted terminal transmits the inspection certificate directly to the State Department of Motor Vehicles database. You can immediately renew your registration online without mailing any paper certificates.
