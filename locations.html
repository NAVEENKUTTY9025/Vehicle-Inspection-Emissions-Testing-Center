Location Questions

A few of the most common questions about visiting our stations.

Walk-ins are welcome at all stations and typically take under 15 minutes. However, you can book an appointment online to guarantee a specific time slot, especially during peak hours.
Most locations offer the full range of services. Some specialty inspections (RV, imported vehicles, antique vehicles) may only be available at select larger stations. Check the services listed on each location card.
Weekend hours vary by location. Most stations are open on Saturdays from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. A few locations have Sunday hours. Check each location card for specific hours.
